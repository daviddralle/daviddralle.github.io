Data & methods

Weather source, coverage & downloads

Cumulative precipitation starts October 1. Medians and ranges compare the same calendar date and exclude years missing any daily rainfall up to that date. A gap stops a cumulative curve for the rest of that year. Curves align by month/day; non-leap years carry February 28 totals on the February 29 position, which is flagged in the comparison download. The full-year view changes the horizontal extent; summary statistics retain the selected year’s through-date.

Full daily record · SI CSV · Original IEM download · Metadata & daily values

Downloads labeled SI retain °C, mm, and m/s. “Download selection” uses the displayed units and includes the source, trace flags, and missing values. Water years run October 1 through September 30. Volumetric water content and water potential retain m³/m³ and MPa in both display systems.

Logger observations & field records

The Raspberry Pi receives Top’s FTPS files, archives their original bytes, builds the dashboard data and publishes updates every 10 minutes when files change. Top includes its own sensors and relayed Bottom tables. Measurement times are preserved exactly as supplied; no time-zone correction, gap filling or calibration is inferred.

Top measures soil every 30 minutes and sap every two hours. Weather summaries are hourly. Daily minimum battery voltage is available only after a daily record is received. Rainfall accumulated since deployment is not a complete water-year total.

The field notebook saves drafts in the current browser and exports JSON for reviewed import on the Pi. It does not upload notes automatically. Original field sheets and photographs belong in the shared Drive field-notes folder; this dashboard does not publish those originals.
