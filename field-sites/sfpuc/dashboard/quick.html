Plot notes

Sap flow uses each sensor’s observed water-year minimum and maximum; changing the window keeps the same scaling. Soil lines show the five 15 cm layers. VPD is estimated from hourly mean air temperature and relative humidity using the saturation vapor pressure relationship.

Surface-installed probes. Moisture represents five 15 cm layers from 0 to 75 cm; profile colors interpolate between layer midpoints and hold the outer half-layers at their measured values. Temperature depths: 3.5, 10, 20, 30, 45, 60 and 75 cm; no extrapolation above the shallowest sensor. T and U share color and moisture axis scales. Blank areas indicate missing observations.
