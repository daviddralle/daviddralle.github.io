How to read the plots

Windows up to 30 days show five-minute observations. Longer windows show daily mean temperature, humidity and wind, daily peak gust, and daily rainfall. All plots share the same time axis; drag to inspect a shorter interval.

Rainfall is the change in the station’s running rain counter between adjacent observations. Missing intervals and unresolved counter resets remain unknown. Cumulative rain starts at zero for each selected window and sums rainfall increments; incomplete totals are marked. Daily means use available samples. Days and timestamps use America/Los_Angeles, including daylight saving time.

The CSV button downloads the displayed series in the selected units.
