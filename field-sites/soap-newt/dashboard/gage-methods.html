Gage processing

Provisional research estimates. Input review: 4 November 2026.

Pressure to staff-gage stage

Read original Solinst XLE timestamps and channel units. Convert pressure to metres of water (1 kPa = 0.101972 m). Coalesce identical observations across downloads; conflicting readings at the same serial number and timestamp become missing. Retain documented sensor deployments. All logger and visit times use fixed PST (UTC−08:00), confirmed by the site operator.

Subtract NEON SOAP station pressure, DP1.00004.001, position 000.025. Use staPresMean only where staPresFinalQF = 0. Treat each 30-minute mean as located at its interval midpoint; interpolate only between consecutive valid midpoints. Never bridge flagged or missing intervals, or extrapolate beyond the pressure record. Sea-level-corrected pressure is not used.

Estimate a constant staff-gage datum offset from the median of paired manual stage minus corrected pressure head, matching times within 8 minutes. This empirical offset combines the sensor datum, atmospheric elevation difference, and any constant sensor bias; it does not identify those contributions separately. Local pressure differences, sensor handling, and channel changes remain sources of error.

Stage to discharge

Fit Q = a(h − h₀)b to manual staff-stage/discharge pairs. Use a robust soft-L1 loss on log discharge, with loss scale 0.5. Bounds are 0.5 ≤ b ≤ 6 and min(h) − 0.5 m ≤ h₀ ≤ min(h) − 0.0001 m. The fitted h₀ is not a surveyed zero-flow elevation. Residual size alone does not remove a measurement.

Exclude missing stage, missing or nonpositive discharge, source-flagged measurements, known invalid conductivity timestamps, and stage/discharge observations more than 60 minutes apart. This last limit is a conservative pairing rule, not evidence that those discharge measurements are wrong. Keep every source row and reason in the downloadable measurement table.

Validate by leaving out one visit date, refitting, and predicting the omitted measurements. Report exp(median(|log(predicted/measured)|)). This measures rating scatter on held-out visits; it is not a prediction interval and does not include all datum, pressure, stage, channel-change, or drainage-area uncertainty. The change-date sheet is empty, so these are single provisional ratings without inferred shift corrections.

Discharge to daily runoff

Evaluate discharge at each valid logger reading before averaging into quarter-hour slots. Publish a daily mean only with at least 87 of 96 valid slots. Convert to equivalent runoff using R = 86.4 Q/A, with Q in m³/s, A in km², and R in mm/day. Areas are 0.872082 km² for Soap and 0.792103 km² for Newt, from the archived watershed polygons. Their correspondence to contributing areas at the gages needs field confirmation.

Omit readings outside the measured rating stage range and before the first or after the last accepted rating-visit date. Daily estimates describe covered intervals; missing intervals are not filled. Gaps do not establish zero flow, and annual water balances should not be computed by treating them as zeros. Pressure QA and rating support limit coverage even where logger data exist.

Known review items

  • Newt, 19 May 2026: conductivity timestamps advance one calendar day per row. The archived near-zero discharge is excluded. The actual sample interval must be recovered before recalculation.
  • Soap, 16 April and 15 May 2025: dilution measurements depart from nearby stage–discharge observations. They remain in the robust fit pending review.
  • Soap, 11 June 2026: one summary row repeats the discharge with a midnight timestamp and no stage. It is not an additional calibration pair.
  • Earlier Soap deployments are not joined across sensor changes. No datum or rating shifts are invented from residuals.

The existing dilution notebook substitutes a 1-second cadence for repeated timestamps and uses the first interval for the entire integral. Future processing should reject ambiguous clocks, match calibration records explicitly, integrate conductivity against verified elapsed time, and check background, tracer-tail coverage, and calibration residuals. Source notebooks and field sheets have not been overwritten.
