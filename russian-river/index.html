Published evidence, explicit assumptions

This atlas combines Sonoma County's existing flood simulations with public lidar and FEMA mapping. It does not yet estimate annual or El Niño-conditioned flood probabilities.

What the numbers mean

Flood color: county-modeled depth above ground at the selected Guerneville gauge stage. It is not depth inside the house.

Floor: 37 ft NAVD88 plus the adjustable step-up. This assumes the floor is near the road-side entrance grade; the entrance itself has not been identified. The actual floor could be outside the ±2 ft sensitivity band.

Water elevation: county flood-depth raster plus the 2013 bare-earth terrain, summarized over the county building footprint. The original hydraulic terrain has not been obtained, so this is an approximate reconstruction.

Under the house: outdoor gear/storage, per the owner's friend. Contents, utilities, access, and structural impacts are not included in the living-floor comparison.

Vertical reference: NAVD88 feet. 2013 data use Geoid12A; 2022 data use Geoid18. Differences in terrain age, geoid realization, location and raster sampling remain part of the uncertainty.
