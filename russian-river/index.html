What the numbers mean

Flood color: county-modeled depth above ground at the selected Guerneville gauge stage. It is not depth inside the house.

Floor: 45.6 ft NAVD88 from C2.b of the September 25, 2018 elevation certificate. Diagram 6 and the photographs show a raised floor above a lower enclosure. C2.b is used as the living-floor reference given the reported storage use below. The floor elevation is fixed to the certificate.

Water elevation: county flood-depth raster plus the 2013 bare-earth terrain, summarized over the county building footprint. The source metadata identify 2015 lidar as the original hydraulic terrain. That terrain has not been obtained, so reconstruction with 2013 lidar is approximate.

Lower enclosure: floor/garage 37.2 ft NAVD88; hot water heater 37.1 ft in the 2018 certificate. Reported storage use does not remove equipment or access exposure. The living-floor comparison does not estimate those losses.

Vertical reference: NAVD88 feet. 2013 data use Geoid12A; 2022 data use Geoid18. Differences in terrain age, geoid realization, location and raster sampling remain part of the uncertainty.
