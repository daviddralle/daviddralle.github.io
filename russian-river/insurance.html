Methods and limitations

The calculation follows the general stage → depth → damage → payout approach described by USACE HEC-FDA. The editable repair amounts are analyst-created placeholders; they are not USACE damage curves or local contractor estimates. NFIP coverage guidance informs which terms need verification; this is not a determination of coverage.

Water elevations come from the atlas’s county depth + 2013 terrain reconstruction at integer gauge stages. Certificate building elevations are fixed. The 2019 waterline check remains unresolved. No new HEC-RAS simulation, flood-frequency fit, or El Niño adjustment is used here. A 45-ft scenario approximates the gauge level of 2019; it does not reconstruct the event.

Model: downstairs loss = allowance × min(1, positive depth / 3 ft). Upstairs loss = editable depth-cost curve, zero at/below the living floor and the first-wetting cost immediately above it; costs are held at the 10-ft value beyond that depth. Eligible loss = each component × its eligible share. Payout = min(limit, max(0, eligible loss − deductible)). Retained loss = total modeled loss − payout. Annual results are probability-weighted sums over the five scenarios; other years receive zero loss. No depreciation, inflation, premium increases, waiting periods, structural failure/erosion losses beyond the allowances, or claims disputes are modeled.
